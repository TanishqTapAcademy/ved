Ready to turn campaigns into journeys and contacts into relationships? This course makes you genuinely proficient in Salesforce Marketing Cloud from your first email send to fully automated, data-driven journeys across email, mobile and social.

What you'll learn

  • Marketing Cloud foundations core features, capabilities and confident navigation of the interface for day-to-day campaign management.
  • Email marketing mastery responsive, on-brand emails with personalisation and dynamic content blocks.
