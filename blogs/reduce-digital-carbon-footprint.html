Reduce your digital carbon footprint with Salesforce: the future of sustainable IT

Every data centre, cloud server and digital transaction burns energy and energy has a carbon cost. Sustainable IT is how you keep growing without growing your emissions.

SS
Sachin Sharma
Salesforce Strategy Lead · Mar 25, 2025 · 6 min read

In a digital-first world we lean on technology more every year and most of that technology runs on energy we never see. The hidden line item on every digital balance sheet is carbon. The good news: with the right platform, growth and decarbonisation stop being a trade-off.

At VedSphere we believe technology and sustainability belong together. We help forward-thinking companies shrink their digital carbon footprint through Sustainable IT practical changes, powered by Salesforce, that cut energy use without slowing the business down.

What is Sustainable IT?

Sustainable IT is the deliberate use of technology to reduce energy consumption, minimise electronic waste, and lower the environmental impact of digital operations. In day-to-day terms it means:

  • Cutting data-storage inefficiencies and redundant copies.
  • Optimising cloud infrastructure so you pay for what you actually use.
  • Putting eco-friendly IT policies into the way teams work.

The goal is simple: maximum performance, minimum impact.

Going green isn't a tax on growth done well, it's the same work as making your systems faster, leaner and cheaper to run.

Why your digital carbon footprint matters

1

Lower energy consumption

Consolidating operations on a carbon-efficient cloud cuts power draw and waste and usually your infrastructure bill alongside it.

2

Compliance & responsibility

Regulators worldwide are pushing for greener practices. Sustainable IT keeps you compliant and builds a credible, socially responsible brand.

3

Stakeholder trust

Customers, investors and talent increasingly choose companies that can prove their environmental commitments with data, not slogans.

How Salesforce drives Sustainable IT

Salesforce isn't only a CRM it's a leader in green cloud technology. Two pieces matter most:

Carbon-neutral cloud computing

Salesforce operates on 100% renewable energy and delivers a carbon-neutral platform, which helps reduce the indirect (Scope 3) emissions that come from your IT services.

Net Zero Cloud

Net Zero Cloud (formerly Sustainability Cloud) turns sustainability from a guess into a measurable program. With it you can:

  • Monitor carbon emissions across the business in one place.
  • Analyse energy usage and spot the biggest reduction opportunities.
  • Set measurable goals and track progress in real time.
  • Drive data-backed decisions on the road to net zero.

How VedSphere helps you build a greener stack

  • Cloud optimisation migrating off energy-hungry legacy systems to a lean, cloud-first setup that cuts consumption significantly.
  • Data efficiency governance best practices that eliminate storage waste, kill redundancy and tighten digital footprint.
  • Net Zero Cloud enablement standing up the tooling so you can track, manage and reduce emissions with confidence.

Sustainable IT is the way forward

Organisations that embrace green technology cut operating costs, meet compliance standards, win over eco-conscious stakeholders, and lead their market with future-ready solutions.
