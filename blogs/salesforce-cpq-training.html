Salesforce CPQ Training

Streamline your sales process, boost efficiency and maximise revenue. A specialised course for professionals who want to master Configure, Price and Quote.

VS
VedSphere Training Team
Live cohort · Online · Certification prep · Dec 3, 2023

Ready to make quoting fast, accurate and profitable? This dedicated CPQ course teaches you to configure complex products, price them intelligently and turn quotes around in minutes — the engine behind a high-velocity sales team.

What you'll learn

  • CPQ foundations — key features and benefits, plus confident navigation for a seamless user experience.
  • Product configuration — setting up and managing product catalogs and building bundles for diverse offerings.
  • Pricing & discounting — dynamic pricing rules and discounts and promotions applied the right way.
  • Quote generation & management — professional, accurate quotes tracked across their full lifecycle.
  • Approval processes — approval workflows that keep quotes compliant with company policy.
  • CRM integration — syncing CPQ data with Salesforce CRM so sales and quoting teams stay aligned.
  • Renewals & subscriptions — managing subscription products and automating renewals for recurring revenue.
  • Reporting & analytics — insightful reports on CPQ performance for data-driven decisions.
